## Decisions the new model must preserve

- **D42:** closing the active live tab selects the next open tab to its
  right, otherwise its left, skipping unopened saved rows. Closing the last
  live tab shows Home and keeps Atlas open. New ordinary web tabs append and
  activate. Reordering stays within saved or ordinary groups. Keep the
  twenty most recent closed **web instances, saved or ordinary**, in memory
  until exit; plugin closures do not enter that history.
- **D43:** Pin means Save. A saved page has a stable saved identity and
  fixed saved title/address, independent of its live tab's fresh identity
  and navigation. Closing unloads the live tab and preserves the saved row.
  Opening a saved row focuses its existing instance or starts a fresh one
  at its saved destination. Saving appends to the saved group. Removing or
  unpinning a saved row keeps any live page at the start of ordinary tabs;
  an unopened removed row simply disappears.
- **Ctrl Shift T:** ordinary closures restore their live metadata and
  position. For a saved closure whose row still exists, focus its existing
  live instance or open its fixed saved destination. If the row was removed,
  history may restore an ordinary page, but never recreate the saved record.
  It restores no live page state or form contents. A temporary documentation
  interpretation excluded saved closures; that was rejected and corrected.
  Do not change the implementation to match any remaining stale wording.
- **D44:** later sidebar UI supports smooth dragging from wide labels to
  favicon-only to hidden, and back. Hidden hover peek and Ctrl Alt S remain.
  Exact thresholds and compact-folder controls await their UI review.
- **D45:** the square sidebar tiles are Favorites, separate from saved rows
  and folders. They can target websites, plugin pages and other supported
  Atlas destinations. They use the normal destination lifecycle; unavailable
  plugins must not pretend to open.

## Implementation and checks at this snapshot

`crates/atlas-core` is a new dependency-free library. `src/tabs.rs` owns
checked `TabId`/`SavedPageId` values, complete tab kinds, owned metadata,
selection, saved/ordinary ordering, save/open/remove operations and closed
history. Only a live `Tab` owns its saved-page association. Saved records
carry no second live identity. `sidebar_rows()` merges the ordered saved
catalogue with the ordered open-saved prefix in linear time without
allocation. Metadata-bearing `Debug` output is redacted; errors contain
only structural values. No database, browser or GUI exists in this slice.
